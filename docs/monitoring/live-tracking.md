# Live Tracking

See where your vehicles are, on a map and in a list grouped by area.

![Live Tracking](../assets/img/live-tracking.png)

## Find a vehicle
1. Open **Monitoring > Live Tracking**.
2. Type in **Search**, or click a group's arrow to open it.
3. Find your vehicle in the list. Its icon shows if it is online or offline.

Click the **A-Z** button to change the sort order.

## On the map
- Each vehicle is shown with its name.
- The legend at the top left explains the colours: green is **Online**, red is **Offline** and orange is **Alarm**.
- A **Locator** button sits at the top of the map.
- The buttons at the top right change the map layers and make the map full screen.
