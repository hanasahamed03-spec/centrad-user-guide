## Vehicles and maps

<div class="cen-gloss" markdown>

- **Vehicle, device, unit**

    The items listed in Live Tracking, Live Video and Asset Management. Each has a device name, and usually a registration.

    See [Live Tracking](monitoring/live-tracking.md)

- **Group**

    A heading that vehicles are listed under, for example *Tamworth*. A group can have a **parent group**.

    See [Asset Management](configurations/asset-management.md)

- **Online, Offline, Alarm**

    The map legend. Green is online, red is offline and orange is alarm.

    See [Live Tracking](monitoring/live-tracking.md)

- **Geofence**

    An area you draw on the map.

    See [Geofence](geofence.md)

</div>

## Video

<div class="cen-gloss" markdown>

- **Channel**

    One camera on a vehicle. Video Playback shows one timeline row for each channel.

    See [Video Playback](video-management/video-playback.md)

- **Stream**

    In Video Playback, the quality of the video: **High quality** or **Low bandwidth**.

    See [Video Playback](video-management/video-playback.md)

- **Subject**

    Optional text that describes a video clip you request.

    See [Video Downloads](video-management/video-downloads.md)

</div>

## Events and the dashboard

<div class="cen-gloss" markdown>

- **Event**

    An entry in the Event Center, for example *Harsh Braking*. It shows a status, device, driver, channel and address.

    See [Event Center](video-management/event-center.md)

- **AI events**

    A dashboard tile that counts events needing review.

    See [Dashboard](dashboard.md)

- **Evidence request**

    A dashboard tile that counts evidence requests, and how many are still processing.

    See [Dashboard](dashboard.md)

- **Fleet health (CEN checks)**

    A dashboard panel that counts **Recording OK**, **Camera faults**, **Device offline** and **GPS lost**.

    See [Dashboard](dashboard.md)

</div>

## Records and checks

<div class="cen-gloss" markdown>

- **Evidence**

    An incident record in the Evidence Locker, with its details and attached files.

    See [Evidence Locker](evidence-locker.md)

- **Template**

    A CEN Check form. Each template shows its forms and the vehicles it is assigned to.

    See [Template](cen-check/templates.md)

- **Completed template**

    A check that has been filled in. It appears under CEN Check > Completed Templates.

    See [Completed Templates](cen-check/completed-templates.md)

- **Notification Hub**

    The list of notifications on the Notifications page. Each has a type, such as *Report*, *Event* or *Service / MOT*.

    See [Notifications](notifications.md)

</div>
