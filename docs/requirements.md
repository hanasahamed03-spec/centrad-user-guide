# Requirements

What you need before you start.

## What you need

<div class="cen-reqs" markdown>

- :material-account-key-outline: **An account**

    A username and password. No account yet? Click **Request access** on the sign-in page.

- :material-web: **A web browser**

    The latest **Chrome**, **Edge** or **Safari**, as the sign-in page recommends.

- :material-lifebuoy: **A way back in**

    Forgot your password? Click **Forgot password?** next to the Password field on the sign-in page.

- :material-monitor-account: **Remember me**

    Tick **Remember me on this device** above the **Sign in** button if you want the portal to remember you on that device.

</div>

## Before you start

<div class="cen-checklist" markdown>

- :material-check-circle-outline: You have your **username** and **password**.
- :material-check-circle-outline: You are using the latest **Chrome**, **Edge** or **Safari**.
- :material-check-circle-outline: You can open the portal's **sign-in page**.

</div>
