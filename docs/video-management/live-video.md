# Live Video

Watch a vehicle's cameras live.

![Live Video](../assets/img/live-video.png)

## Start live video
1. Open **Video Management > Live Video**.
2. Find the vehicle in the list on the left, or on the map. Use **Search** and **A-Z** to find it quickly.
3. **Double-click the device.**
4. The video appears in the panel on the right, marked **LIVE**.
5. Click **Previous** or **Next** to change channel.

Before you start, the panel says *Double-click a device to start live video*.

## On the page
- The speaker icon controls the sound. The camera icon is next to it.
- The red arrows between the panels fold the list and the map away, to give the video more room.
