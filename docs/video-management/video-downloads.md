# Video Downloads

Request a video clip, then watch it from your list of requests.

![Video Downloads](../assets/img/video-downloads.png)

## Request a clip
Under **Request Video**:

1. Choose the **Device Name**.
2. Choose the **Date and time** the clip should start.
3. Set the **Duration Minutes**.
4. If you like, type a **Subject** to describe the clip.
5. Tick **Email me the video link when ready** if you want an email.
6. Click **Request Footage**.

## Watch your clip
Your requests are listed under **Downloaded Videos**. When **Status** shows **Completed**, click **Play**.

## Find and save requests
- Type in **Search downloads** to search the list.
- Click **Filters** to narrow the list.
- Click **Export** to save the list.

The columns are **Action**, **Status**, **Device Name**, **No. of Channel(s)**, **Date From**, **Date To**, **Subject** and **Created Date**.
