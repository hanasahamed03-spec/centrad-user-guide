# Add evidence

![Add evidence](../assets/img/evidence-create.png)

1. Open **Evidence Locker** and click **Create New**.
2. Fill in the fields marked with a star, and choose the dates.
3. Under **Attach file**, add your files.
4. Click **Save**.

Full details: [Evidence Locker](../evidence-locker.md).
